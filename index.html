The short version

  • The suspensions started when we began mailing the old SendGrid list. Those first emails lose 3.6% of people to unsubscribes.
  • People get safer with every email they stay for. By the fifth email it's 0.5%.
  • So the fix is pacing, not a smarter model: mix a small slice of new people into each campaign.
  • JEV scored every contact in minutes for about $2. It couldn't predict who would unsubscribe, and neither could anything else.
  • Every contact now has its tier in Brevo, so each group is one segment away.

Why the account keeps getting suspended

The SendGrid backlog

June and July campaigns ran at 0.1% to 0.9% unsubscribes. The September warm-ups hit 2.5% to 4.9%. 88% of recent unsubscribes came from people getting their first Brevo email.

The SendGrid segments are broken

Segments 24 to 30 contradict each other. More than half of "engaged 90 days" is also in "never engaged". Audiences built from them are close to random.

What JEV did, and didn't do

Where it helped

  • Scored all 118,769 contacts in about 3 minutes for $2.31.
  • Cheap enough to re-run after we fixed the data, which we did.
  • Showed clearly that the backlog can't be predicted, so nobody should waste time trying.

Where it didn't

  • On clean data it was a coin flip (0.54), so it doesn't set the tiers.
  • On the first run it was fooled by a field that changes after someone unsubscribes.
  • The real limit is the data: most contacts have never been emailed from Brevo, so there's no behaviour to read.

Bottom line: JEV is 5x to 40x cheaper than an LLM for reading a whole list, and fast enough to re-run after every fix. It just can't find signal that isn't there.

The send plan

We don't know Brevo's exact suspension line. What we do know: sends at 0.1% to 0.9% went fine, and sends at 2.5% to 4.9% got rejected. So aim for 1% or less per campaign.

  1. Next two campaigns: the 4,871 safe contacts, plus half of wave 1 each time. That should land around 1.2%.
  2. Then wave 2: add it in slices small enough to stay under 1%. The slices can grow as people stick around.
  3. Give it three days: at least three days between emails to the same person. The Labor Day warm-ups went out on 4, 5 and 7 Sept.
  4. Stop rule: over 1% on a send? Halve the next slice. Anyone who marks spam comes off right away.
  5. Never mail EXCLUDE, SUPPRESS or NEW-EXCLUDE, and don't build audiences from segments 24 to 30.
Method notes
  • Backtest: features as of 1 Aug 2026; label = unsubscribed or complained after that. 11,276 contacts, 629 unsubscribes.
  • Hidden from the backtest because they change after someone unsubscribes: WooCommerce subscription status, lists #63 and #64, and live engagement segments.
  • Brevo keeps per-contact email history from June 2026 only, so "never mailed" means not mailed from Brevo since then.
  • Order data looks incomplete for many WooCommerce buyers without opt-in, so some repeat buyers may sit in EXCLUDE.
  • JEV: typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917 via OpenRouter, 100 contacts per call, 3 questions each.
  • The pull took about 2.5 hours through the API. Campaign recipient exports would cut that to minutes next time.
