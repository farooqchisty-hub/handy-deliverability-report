Handy: who to mail, who to hold

Rebuilt on every Brevo email sent since 2021. 42,012 subscribed contacts have been emailed at least once. 7,823 of them are safe to mail now. 76,579 have never had a Brevo email, and their first one is where the unsubscribes come from.

Source: read-only pull from the Brevo account, plus recipient exports for all 308 campaigns (300 sent, 7 rejected, 1 suspended): 1.08 million recipient rows. Nothing was sent. The only change in the account is one contact field, UNSUB_RISK_TIER. This page shows totals only; contact-level files are shared privately.

What changed from v3. v3 only read the last 90 days of history, which is the API's default window. It's not a limit on what Brevo keeps. Leesha spotted it. v4 uses every campaign since 2021, including 8 rejected or suspended campaigns that still reached 20,981 people. Contacts with history went from 14,189 to 42,012. The earlier ramp waves were dropped, because their signals didn't hold up in same-campaign comparisons (section 4).

The short version

  • 80% of unsubscribes since 1 Aug came from people getting their first-ever Brevo email. They unsubscribe at 3.1% per email.
  • People who clicked in the last year unsubscribe at 0.6% to 0.7%. That's the safe core: 4,447 contacts.
  • Risk falls with every email someone stays for: 3.4% on the first, 0.6% from the sixth on (2026 sends).
  • Rejected campaigns still went out. Eight of them reached 20,981 people, at 1.1% to 4.7% unsubscribes.
  • 86% of first-ever emails since 1 Aug went to WooCommerce buyers with no opt-in on record.
  • JEV, full history: 0.56, about the same as simple history tiers (0.58). Nothing predicts first-ever emails.

6. What JEV did, and didn't do

Where it helped

  • Scored every row fast and cheaply: $4.90 across three runs, including a full-list run of 118,769 contacts in about 3 minutes.
  • Cheap enough to re-run every time the data was fixed, which happened twice.
  • Confirmed that first-ever emails can't be predicted, which is what led to holding that pool.

Where it didn't

  • On full history it scored 0.56, level with simple history tiers and below a basic statistical model (0.64).
  • On first-ever emails it scored 0.47, no better than chance.
  • In run 1 it was fooled by a field that changes after someone unsubscribes.

Accuracy runs from 0.5 (coin flip) to 1.0 (perfect), measured on the same 14,498 contacts and 620 real unsubscribes (full history). LLM costs are estimated for the same 130,045 rows from measured token counts at list prices, 50 contacts per call; "Batched" is the Batch API at half price. JEV ran three times as the data was fixed, $4.90 in total. The same three runs with an LLM would have cost roughly $25 to $190.

Bottom line: JEV is 5x to 40x cheaper than an LLM for reading a whole list, and fast enough to re-run after every fix. It just can't find signal that isn't there.

7. The send plan

Brevo's exact suspension line isn't known. Engaged sends in June and July ran at 0.1% to 0.9% and went through. The August and September sends with first-timers ran at 2% to 5% and got rejected. So aim for 1% or less per campaign.

  1. Core audience: SEND (4,447) in every campaign.
  2. Add SEND-SMALL (3,376) in batches of 1,000 or fewer, mixed with SEND.
  3. Win back HOLD (32,875) slowly: about 1,000 per campaign mixed with SEND keeps the blend near 0.8%. Drop anyone who doesn't open after two tries.
  4. Don't put HOLD-NO-HISTORY in regular campaigns. If the business wants to reach them, use a separate, small, slow warm-up with the lower-risk group first, ideally after the SendGrid export.
  5. Three days minimum between emails to the same person. The Labor Day warm-ups went out on 4, 5, 7 and 8 Sept.
  6. Stop rule: over 1% on a send, pause the add-on slices next time. Anyone who marks spam comes off immediately.
  7. Confirm consent for WooCommerce buyers without opt-in before mailing them further.
  8. Look at Quickie Launch 2 group 2, which ran at 1.1% against 3.3% to 3.6% for the same audience. Whatever was different there is the most useful lead in this data.
Method notes
  • History: recipient exports for all 308 campaigns (sent, rejected, suspended), 1,078,064 rows, 53,553 distinct emails. Apple Mail privacy opens are separated from real opens.
  • Backtest: history as of 1 Aug 2026; outcome = unsubscribed or complained on any email after that. 14,498 contacts, 27,077 emails, 620 unsubscribes or complaints.
  • Hidden from the backtest because they change after an unsubscribe: WooCommerce subscription status, audience lists #63 and #64, live engagement segments.
  • First-ever email analysis: 13,575 contacts whose first Brevo email was after 1 Aug; signals compared within campaign (Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio across 9 campaigns with 300+ first-timers).
  • Accuracy is AUC: 0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect. JEV (typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917) 0.56; history tiers 0.58; logistic model 0.64 on a 30% holdout.
  • Earlier versions: v1 to v3 read only 90 days of history, because that's the per-contact API's default window. Corrected in v4.
